# ESADE Brand Book
## Visual Identity Guidelines for Presentations

---

## 🎓 About ESADE

ESADE Business School, founded in 1958 in Barcelona, Spain, is a leading global institution for business and law education. The institution's positioning is centered around **"Do Good. Do Better."** - emphasizing meaningful change through innovation and social commitment.

## 🎨 Brand Colors

- **RGB:** `0, 46, 93`
- **Usage:** Primary brand color, headers, backgrounds
- **Description:** Dark, elegant navy that represents trust and professionalism

- **Hex:** `#000000`

- **Usage:** Text, logo, emphasis

## 🔤 Typography

### Primary Typeface: Esade Font (Custom)

**Characteristics:**
- Custom serif display typeface
- Three font weights available
- Stencil details
- Innovative forms with strong character
- Used exclusively for headlines and titles

**Font Weights:**
- Light
- Regular
- Bold

**Usage:**
- Main titles (H1)
- Section headers
- Logo wordmark
- Brand statements

### Secondary Typeface: Mabry Pro

**Characteristics:**
- Modern sans-serif
- Excellent readability
- Clean and geometric
- From Colophon Foundry

**Usage:**
- Body text
- Subheadings (H2-H6)
- Lists and bullet points
- Captions and notes

### Web-Safe Fallback Stack

Since custom fonts may not be available in all contexts:

**Serif Fallback:**
```css
font-family: 'Esade Font', 'Georgia', 'Times New Roman', serif;
```

**Sans-Serif Fallback:**
```css
font-family: 'Mabry Pro', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', 'Helvetica Neue', Arial, sans-serif;
```

## 📐 Logo Guidelines

### Logo Characteristics

- **Style:** Lowercase wordmark "esade"
- **Design:** Inspired by 1958 original, modernized
- **Characteristics:** Bold, confident, heritage-inspired
- **Typography:** Custom Esade Font

### Logo Usage

**Primary Logo:**
- Black on white background (preferred)
- White on dark backgrounds
- Minimum size: 120px width

**Clear Space:**
- Maintain space around logo equal to height of lowercase "e"
- No other elements within clear space zone

**Do's:**
- Use official logo files only
- Maintain aspect ratio
- Use on high-contrast backgrounds

**Don'ts:**
- Don't stretch or distort
- Don't add effects or shadows
- Don't place on busy backgrounds
- Don't change colors (except approved variants)

## 🎯 Design Principles

### 1. Human-Centered
Place people at the center of all communications. Use authentic photography showing real students, faculty, and scenarios.

### 2. Bold & Confident
Use strong typography, clear hierarchies, and confident statements. ESADE is a leader.

### 3. Modern Heritage
Balance traditional excellence (1958 founding) with contemporary innovation and digital fluency.

### 4. Professional Excellence
Maintain high standards in all visual communications. Quality reflects institutional values.

### 5. Do Good. Do Better.
Every design should reflect the commitment to meaningful change and social responsibility.

## 📊 Presentation Guidelines

### Slide Structure

**Title Slide:**
- Large title in Esade Font
- Subtitle in Mabry Pro
- Navy blue background with white text
- Optional: Kumera gold accent

**Content Slides:**
- White or light gray background
- Black text (Mabry Pro)
- Navy blue headers (Esade Font)
- Generous white space

**Section Dividers:**
- Full-bleed navy blue or electric blue
- Large white text
- Minimal content

**Closing Slide:**
- ESADE logo
- Contact information
- Thank you message

### Color Combinations

**Professional (Default):**
- Background: White (#FFFFFF)
- Text: Black (#000000)
- Headers: Navy Blue (#002E5D)
- Accents: Kumera Gold (#83691F)

**Dynamic:**
- Background: Navy Blue (#002E5D)
- Text: White (#FFFFFF)
- Accents: Electric Blue (#00A7E1)
- Highlights: Coral (#FF6B6B)

**Innovation:**
- Background: Light Gray (#F5F5F5)
- Text: Black (#000000)
- Accents: Emerald Green (#4ECDC4)
- Highlights: Electric Blue (#00A7E1)

## ♿ Accessibility

### Color Contrast
- Maintain WCAG AA standard (4.5:1 minimum for text)
- Navy blue on white: Excellent contrast
- Never use light colors on light backgrounds
- Test all color combinations

### Typography
- Minimum 18pt for body text in presentations
- 32pt+ for headings
- Avoid all-caps for long text
- Ensure proper line spacing (1.5x)

### Content
- Use alt text for images
- Provide captions for videos
- Structure content with proper headings
- Keep animations subtle and purposeful

---

## 🌐 Digital Applications

### Web Presentations
- Optimize for 16:9 aspect ratio (1920x1080)
- Test on multiple screen sizes
- Ensure fast load times
- Use web-safe font fallbacks

### Social Media
- Adapt brand colors for platform requirements
- Maintain logo visibility at small sizes
- Keep text large and readable on mobile
